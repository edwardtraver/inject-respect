INJECT-RESPECT: Elements of the history when evaluating for complications of injection drug use

Elements of the history when evaluating for complications of injection drug use.

Thirteen domains of questions for taking a history of injection drug use practices, to better inform diagnosis and treatment planning. Developed through a multi-phase, iterative process involving expert clinicians and people who inject drugs.
